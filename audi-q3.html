Next-Gen Audi Q3 Revealed: Hybrid Power & Digital Cockpits

By THE OVERWORLD Team • Sep 13, 2026

Audi has unveiled the next-generation Audi Q3, bringing split Digital Stage cockpit screens, dynamic OLED matrix headlights, and aggressive front grill styling.

Under the hood, the new lineup offers upgraded mild-hybrid engines as well as plug-in hybrid options delivering extended electric-only range for city driving.

Inside, the car comes equipped with full Level 2 ADAS autonomous safety suites and an AI driver assistant fully integrated into the main infotainment panel.
