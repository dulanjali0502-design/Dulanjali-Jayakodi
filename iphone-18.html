Apple Officially Announces iPhone 18 Pro & Foldable Ultra

By THE OVERWORLD Team • Sep 12, 2026

Apple has officially dropped its newest smartphone flagship line: the iPhone 18 Pro alongside the long-rumored foldable iPhone Ultra.

The new line features the TSMC 2nm A20 Pro chip, bringing unprecedented power efficiency and massive local AI processing speeds directly on device.

Additionally, the camera system upgrades to a mechanical variable-aperture lens setup, giving mobile photographers real optical depth-of-field control.
